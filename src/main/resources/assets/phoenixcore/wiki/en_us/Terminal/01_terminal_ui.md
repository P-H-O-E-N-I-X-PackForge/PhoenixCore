# Research Terminal

Right-click a Research Terminal to open it. The screen has three parts: a **sidebar** of research trees, the **canvas** showing the active tree, and a **bar along the bottom** with the team's stored data.

## Sidebar

- Trees are grouped under headings (Foundations, Discipline, Dispensations, Chronicle, Space). Each row shows how many nodes you have researched, and a green dot when something in it can be researched now.
- **Search** (`/` or `Ctrl+F`) looks through node titles and lore across every visible tree. Pick a result to jump to the node.
- **Ready** lists every node you can research right now, affordable ones first.
- **Pinned** nodes sit at the top. Pin one with the star in its viewer.
- The triangle at the top (or `Tab`) collapses the sidebar to a rail.

## Canvas

Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Zoom far out for a plain overview. The card in the corner summarises the active tree; click its header to collapse it.

## Node viewer

Click a node to open it. It starts as a card over the canvas; the square button (or `F`) expands it to fullscreen, where it splits into **Record**, **Requirements** and **Yield**.

- `R` or `Enter` researches the node.
- The list button shows what the node requires and what it unlocks; click an entry to open that node.
- The star pins the node.

## Commands

- `/conflux view` - open a detached terminal view
- `/conflux editor` - open the tree editor
- `/conflux discipline` - open the discipline picker
- `/conflux wiki` - open this wiki

## Themes

The terminal follows the shared Phoenix theme. Use the **Theme** button in the top bar to change it.
